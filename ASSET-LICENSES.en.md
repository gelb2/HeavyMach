## Publication scope to obtain

- Store and publish code and assets together in the GitHub repository, history, and LFS.
- Let visitors clone, fork, or download the project, then build and play it on their own devices.
- Establish the scope for sharing modified versions and how to credit the creators.
- Use game captures, GIFs, and videos in articles about development.
- Treat free App Store distribution as a separate permission item.

This document grants no right to use the original assets in other games or sell them separately. When a reply arrives, link the covered files and versions, granting party, permission date, duration, conditions, and evidence here. [Publication and distribution preparation](docs/public/github-licenses-and-badges-20261028.md). The [rights manifest](docs/rights-manifest.json) records status by path.
