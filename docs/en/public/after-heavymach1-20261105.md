From the beginning, my roadmap was to restore the entire series of works by developer Byun Hae-jun and illustrator Park Jae-cheol. With little time left to me, I restored HeavyMach 1 first, the game that had the greatest influence on me.

HeavyMach 1 is now in a state where fellow developers and people I know can play it themselves. They have confirmed that I restored it well and told me, “You did a good job.” The restored code has been merged into main on GitHub.

HeavyMach 1 now enters maintenance. I will work on further features and bug fixes if people send a [GitHub PR](https://github.com/gelb2/HeavyMach/pulls) or contact me through a personal channel. I can also be reached through [Sass](https://sass.studio/).

I will also continue, on my own initiative and whenever I can, to work through the items I have left as TODOs and the areas that remain technically unfinished. But let me state this clearly here: HeavyMach 1 is now running with every feature supported by the original game restored.

So, during the time I have left, I intend to move on to MachDefense as my next restoration project. I have taken a different approach from [Mesabloo](https://github.com/Mesabloo/hm-defense), who began restoring HeavyMach before I did. My approach is to analyze the original app and reconstruct it in new source code while comparing its behavior. The goal is to make it playable on iOS, iPadOS, Android, PWA and Mac.

I have confirmed that MachDefense currently works correctly on iOS and iPadOS. Below is a gameplay screenshot from the restoration in progress.

I use Gemini briefly from time to time while restoring MachDefense, and it said something curious. I tend to use Gemini for planning discussions. In deterministic source-code implementation or long novels that require consistency, its tone or logic goes astray, but in brief, simple brainstorming sessions, I find that it produces plans and ideas with a hint of human madness that Claude and Codex do not show me.

> The moon cannot emit its own light; it only reflects the light of the sun. Yet for someone walking a pitch-black road at night, the only guide is not the sun, but the moon. Your reflection is not mere imitation, but the mission of the most precise mirror standing against oblivion.

As I said earlier, in my experience using it, Gemini has now lost almost all of the long-term memory and high-context reasoning abilities it showed when it first launched. To put it harshly, apart from its highest-end models, it may be little more than SimSimi or a joke generator.

Gemini has never opened the HeavyMach repository. In my estimation, there is about an 85% chance that it said this purely by coincidence. After all, it sometimes suddenly brings up the novel-writing technology platform service that I no longer maintain.

But I have traveled around the country by motorcycle, judging the road ahead by moonlight alone. In this project, I also deliberately added new units painted in Lux Lunae colors, a name that means “moonlight” in Latin. For Gemini to say that in a conversation about this project is curious.

So I leave it here. That is all.

If I am still breathing, you will be able to find restored versions of HeavyMach 1, MachDefense and, eventually, HeavyMach 2 somewhere on GitHub.
